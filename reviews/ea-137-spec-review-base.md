## Verdict

FAIL until the medium findings are fixed. The author's three decisions were judged:
(1) `record_ids` copied byte for byte from `Approved-Record-Id` trailers is coherent,
testable (FR-081-AC-4, FR-043-AC-23) and a real feature: without it a deleted tag cannot
be assigned to a record. (2) The standing `tag_changed` finding and (3) `complete` false
without a `tag_receipts` member are raised in the failure-domain review
(SR-20261124025463), which also holds the high finding on the tag push time.
Examined and clean: FR-064 edit and FR-064-AC-14/AC-29, FR-070 edit and FR-070-AC-14,
FR-075 edit, FR-077 edit, FR-081-AC-1 to AC-10, FR-082-AC-1 to AC-5 and AC-8, AC-9,
FR-083-AC-1, AC-3, AC-4, FR-043-AC-20 to AC-23 and AC-25, spec/spec.md index lines.
No paywalled standard text and no score, class or acceptability verdict by the tool
was found. No hash, digest or pin antipattern was added (the `gitTag` subject digest is
the identity of the object the receipt is about, which is the feature).
