## Verdict

FAIL (one medium). Failure modes for the snapshot (unmakeable, re-hash mismatch, ref change during a run) all fail closed to unverifiable, never signed_bad or approved, and the source-ref move/delete boundary is covered by FR-041-AC-23 and TC-1011. TC-1017's no-public-approval-tag fixture is an open EA-129 anomaly outside this diff and is not reviewed as passing.
